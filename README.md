### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Junior Data Analyst at Reunert Connect** and a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** who graduated **Cum Laude** in Computer Science from the **Tshwane University of Technology (TUT)**. I specialize in building scalable web platforms, automated analytics pipelines, and hardware-to-cloud IoT systems that solve real operational problems.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Enterprise data analytics pipelines, **SAP Business One** ERP reporting, and applied AI software engineering at **Reunert Connect**.
- 🛠️ **Core focus:** Bridging **C# / ASP.NET Core**, **React / TypeScript**, **SQL**, **AI (ML.NET & Semantic Kernel)**, and **ESP32 IoT telemetry**.
- 🏆 **Recognition:** **Best Overall Intern**, **Best Overall System**, and **Best Website Application** (ICEP Awards); National Recognition at the **MICTSETA National Skills Challenge Hackathon**; **TUT Top Achiever** & **2026 Summit Keynote Speaker**.
- 🤝 **Open to collaborating on:** Full-stack enterprise systems, data engineering, applied AI automation, and embedded IoT solutions.
